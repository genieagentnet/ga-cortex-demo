# The sectors

The home page names six sectors as trusted by growing teams.[^genieagent-home]

1. Home services.
2. Dental and medical.
3. Real estate.
4. Legal.
5. Automotive.
6. Hospitality.

All six buy the same core system: the [Voice Agent](/services/voice-agent.md)
answering every call, and [Lead Capture + CRM](/services/lead-capture-crm.md)
capturing every lead. Scripts, hours, and booking rules are configured per
client under [How it works](/process/how-it-works.md).
