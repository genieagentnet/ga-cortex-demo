# Overview

Every [Genie Agent](/company/overview.md) engagement follows four steps.[^genieagent-home]

# The steps

1. Audit: map the client's call flows, tools, and lead sources to find exactly where revenue leaks out today. This is the [AI Readiness Audit](/services/ai-readiness-audit.md), delivered within 10 business days.
2. Design: script the voice agent, connect the CRM, and define routing rules, tailored to how the client actually works.
3. Launch: go live in 2 to 4 weeks, test against real calls, and tune the agent until it sounds like the client's best representative. From this point [Escalation and handoff](/runbooks/escalation-and-handoff.md) governs live calls.
4. Optimize: the client gets a live dashboard and a monthly review, and conversion keeps improving month over month, measured against [Speed to lead](/metrics/speed-to-lead.md).

No technical staff are needed on the client side; Genie Agent handles setup,
integration, and tuning, and the client gets a simple dashboard with no code
required.
