# Commitments

1. Data is encrypted in transit, and access is limited to what is needed to run the client's agents.[^genieagent-home]
2. Data handling and compliance are reviewed as part of every [AI Readiness Audit](/services/ai-readiness-audit.md).
3. Every call is recorded and transcribed, and callers are told the call is recorded. Recordings, transcripts, and lead details are used only to run the service: booking, follow-up, and quality review.[^genieagent-support]
4. Caller information is never sold, and mobile information is never sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes; the full policy is the published Privacy Policy.[^genieagent-privacy]
