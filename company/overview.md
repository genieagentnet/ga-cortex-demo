# Overview

Genie Agent LLC, presented publicly as GenieAgent, is an AI agency and
automation studio. Its promise is stated on the home page: AI agents that
never miss a call, a lead, or an opportunity.[^genieagent-home]

The company sells three services that work as one system:
[AI Readiness Audit](/services/ai-readiness-audit.md) finds where AI will pay
off first, [Voice Agent](/services/voice-agent.md) answers every call around
the clock, and [Lead Capture + CRM](/services/lead-capture-crm.md) turns
every conversation into a clean, followed-up lead. The engagement follows the
four-step process in [How it works](/process/how-it-works.md), and the plans
are listed in [Plans and pricing](/services/plans-and-pricing.md).

Genie Agent serves the six sectors in
[Industries served](/industries/industries-served.md), and its operating
policies for texting and data handling are in
[SMS program](/policies/sms-program.md) and
[Data security](/policies/data-security.md).
