# Decision

[Genie Agent](/company/overview.md) is built around one decision: never miss
a call. The [Voice Agent](/services/voice-agent.md) answers every call in
under a second, around the clock.[^genieagent-home]

# Reasoning stated on the website

1. Missed calls go to voicemail, and most callers just try the next business instead of leaving a message.
2. The faster a new lead hears back, the more likely they are to convert. Seconds beat hours.
3. Every missed call and manual CRM entry is time the team could spend closing instead of chasing.

The measurable form of this decision is [Speed to lead](/metrics/speed-to-lead.md).
