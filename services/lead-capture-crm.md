# Overview

Lead Capture + CRM is the third service of [Genie Agent](/company/overview.md).
Every call, form, and chat becomes a clean, enriched contact in the client's
CRM, with zero manual data entry.[^genieagent-home]

# What it does

1. Auto-syncs leads to HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, GoHighLevel, and most other CRMs, through native integrations or Zapier and webhooks. If it has an API, it can be connected.
2. Sends instant SMS and email follow-up, within seconds; the follow-up target is defined in [Speed to lead](/metrics/speed-to-lead.md).
3. Scores and routes each lead to the right person.
4. Receives the leads captured by the [Voice Agent](/services/voice-agent.md) on every call.

Text follow-up is sent only under the consent rules in the
[SMS program](/policies/sms-program.md).
