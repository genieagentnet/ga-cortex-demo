# Overview

The AI Readiness Audit is the entry service of [Genie Agent](/company/overview.md)
and the first step of [How it works](/process/how-it-works.md). It is a
structured deep-dive into the client's workflows, tools, and data.[^genieagent-home]

# What it includes

1. Workflow and tech-stack assessment.
2. Opportunity and ROI scoring.
3. Data and compliance readiness check, applying the standards in [Data security](/policies/data-security.md).
4. A prioritized 90-day roadmap.
5. A 60-minute strategy readout.

# Terms

The audit is a one-time engagement priced at $1,500, delivered within 10
business days; see [Plans and pricing](/services/plans-and-pricing.md). The
roadmap it produces is what the [Voice Agent](/services/voice-agent.md) and
[Lead Capture + CRM](/services/lead-capture-crm.md) builds are scoped from.
