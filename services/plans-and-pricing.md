# Overview

Genie Agent publishes three plans. There are no long contracts; clients start
with an audit and expand when they see the return.[^genieagent-home]

# The plans

1. Audit, $1,500 one-time: full readiness assessment, ROI and opportunity scoring, a 90-day roadmap, and a 60-minute strategy readout. See [AI Readiness Audit](/services/ai-readiness-audit.md).
2. Voice Agent, $999 a month: 24/7 AI voice receptionist, appointment booking and reminders, call transcripts and summaries, CRM lead sync included, setup and onboarding included. See [Voice Agent](/services/voice-agent.md).
3. Growth, custom pricing: everything in Voice Agent, plus multi-location and call routing, custom automations and workflows, a dedicated success manager, and monthly optimization reviews.

Pricing for [Lead Capture + CRM](/services/lead-capture-crm.md) is included
in the Voice Agent plan as CRM lead sync, and scoped custom under Growth.
