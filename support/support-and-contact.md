# Contact

1. Email: hello@genieagent.net, monitored during support hours.
2. Phone: +1 (240) 690-0800.
3. Clients can also call their own assistant line at any time; the assistant can take a message, book a callback, or transfer urgent calls.[^genieagent-support]

# Hours and response

Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern, excluding
U.S. federal holidays. The first response target is within one business day
for standard requests, one of the measures in
[Speed to lead](/metrics/speed-to-lead.md). Urgent service issues, an
assistant not answering calls, are the top priority during support hours. The
AI assistants themselves operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week; the hours
above are for the human team.

# Reporting a problem

Include the date and approximate time of the call or issue with your timezone,
the phone number that called or was called, what you expected and what
happened instead, and any notification you received. With those four items the
team can usually find the call recording, the transcript, and the system
record within minutes.
